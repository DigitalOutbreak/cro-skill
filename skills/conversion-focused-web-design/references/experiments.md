# Decide what is worth testing

1. Define eligible audience, exposure, assignment unit, numerator, denominator, conversion window and deduplication key. For lead gen prefer qualified leads or booked jobs per eligible visitor. For commerce pair paid orders with revenue or contribution per visitor.
2. Walk a synthetic conversion from assignment to acceptance. Refresh and retry. Reconcile with a trusted CRM/order system using matching dates, timezone and outcome maturity. Keep personal contact details out of analytics. Exclude staff and bots consistently.
3. Locate the consequential loss by source, device, intent and step. Exits can mean successful phone handoffs. Use appropriately consented and redacted recordings, interviews with buyers and nonbuyers, surveys and support notes. Ask “What almost stopped you?” and compare the answer with observed behavior.
4. Rank candidates by reachable audience, economic value, evidence quality, effort and reversibility. Repair broken paths first. A speculative high-reach change may deserve research before implementation. Explain the ranking.
5. Examine the offer's perceived benefits and costs, message relevance, clarity, anxiety, competing tasks and genuine reasons to act now. Consider a meaningfully different proposition when evidence points to offer fit.

## Experiment card

For [eligible audience], [observed barrier and source] suggests [mechanism]. Changing [control to treatment] should change [primary outcome per unit] because [reason]. Identify evidence that would contradict this explanation.

Predeclare stable assignment unit, allocation, primary metric, minimum worthwhile absolute effect, baseline, guardrails, statistical method, power/sample target, business cycles, attribution lag and maximum duration. Randomize concurrently and retain assignment across visits. Check allocation imbalance, missing exposures, variant flicker and CRM joins. Stop for predeclared harm or integrity failures. For fixed-horizon tests, wait for the planned sample and mature outcomes before reading a winner.

At 5% baseline and 6% target, a two-sided fixed-horizon design with 5% significance and 80% power needs roughly 8,200 independent visitors per arm under equal allocation. Confirm the calculation for the actual design. At 500 eligible visitors per month this takes nearly 33 months before lag. Favor observed task sessions, lost-lead interviews and a reversible rollout. Annotate spend, staffing and seasonality. A pre/post comparison remains descriptive.

Report raw counts, rates, absolute percentage-point change, relative lift and an uncertainty interval. Check cancellations, spam, margin and task success. Ship only when the predeclared evidence and business criteria pass. Roll back harm or label uncertainty inconclusive. Treat exploratory subgroup patterns as new hypotheses. A bundled treatment cannot identify which individual change worked.

When sources disagree, report the count difference and scope. Compare record IDs, channel inclusion and reporting windows before labeling extras as duplicates. A diagnostic click and a durable lead may both be accurate counts of different events.
