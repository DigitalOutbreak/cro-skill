# Write the offer and close the loop

Map the buying decision in this order, adjusting for visitor readiness. What brought me here? Can you do this for someone like me? What will happen, what will it cost or require, and why should I trust you? What do I do now? Put the answer where the question arises. Let ready visitors act early and repeat the same primary action after the largest objection is answered.

| Weak component | Rewrite or design move |
| --- | --- |
| “Solutions for a better tomorrow” | Name the work and audience. “Payroll approval for multi-location restaurants” gives a manager a reason to keep reading. |
| Feature list without consequences | Pair mechanism and outcome. “Compare hours across locations before approving payroll” explains what the comparison changes. |
| Testimonial carousel beneath the footer | Place a permissioned, relevant customer account next to the claim it supports. Identify source and scope. Without proof, describe the process in verifiable steps. |
| “Get started” opens a sales form | Say “Request a walkthrough.” Explain who follows up and what information they need. Use “Book” only for a flow that confirms a slot. |
| Hidden cost or commitment | Explain pricing basis, inclusions, exclusions and next commitment before asking for contact details. Link full terms where the visitor decides. |

Use visitor language from interviews, search queries and support logs. Keep a claim ledger with claim, source and verification date. Omit unsupported counts, badges, review stars, guarantees, scarcity and availability. Do not fabricate customers or preselect optional consent.

### Form contract

For each field record who uses it and when. Collect the minimum that enables the promised next step. Gate serviceability early when exclusion is common. Defer detailed addresses, budgets or uploads until needed. Use persistent labels, required/optional indicators, suitable input modes and contextual error text. Provide a phone or asynchronous alternative when appropriate.

Specify idle, invalid, pending, server failure and accepted states. Preserve inputs after errors. Associate messages to fields and move focus or announce status appropriately. Make retries safe. Display success only after durable acceptance. Give the next step, contact route and verified response expectations. When an integration is absent, mark the prototype clearly and avoid a simulated live success.

**Checkout application.** If delivery charges surprise buyers, surface a shipping estimate before payment. Measure completed paid orders per eligible cart, with margin and refund guardrails. A rise in checkout starts alone does not resolve the objection.

### Verify the handoff

Inspect the rendered control's submitted name, form method, handler and CRM field mapping. Native submission skips unnamed controls. Inspect the actual network payload on staging because JavaScript handlers may construct it separately. Use synthetic contact data, check that the URL and analytics omit personal details, and trace exactly one accepted request through to the promised callback or booking.
